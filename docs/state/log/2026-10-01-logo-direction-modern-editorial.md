## What changed

The user likes the editorial personality and explicitly wants the identity to also feel
modern and appropriate for an AI-powered intelligence product. This refines the discussion
in `2026-10-01-logo-direction-discussion.md`; no specific symbol, palette or execution has
been selected, and no logo has been created.

Recommended design approach, still a proposal:

- Combine a contemporary editorial wordmark with a compact, precisely constructed symbol.
- Explore a custom lowercase d with a sculpted exterior, geometric interior and one
  distinctive cut; also explore a restrained condensation or synthesis symbol.
- Start with ink and warm white; use a concentrated blue accent to add a digital character.
- Let the typography, geometry, color and eventual restrained interaction work together.
  A static logo alone cannot unambiguously communicate AI capability.
- Judge each candidate at favicon, extension-toolbar and sidebar sizes as well as in its
  full wordmark arrangement. Keep detail substantial enough to survive those sizes.
